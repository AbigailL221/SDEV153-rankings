#3 Catnap

Poppy Playtime




Catnap uses Poppy Playtime's style of using typically happy sources like toys for its monster designs.
The smiling face highlights the contrast that the game commonly uses to make the creatures even scarier.
During the game, Catnap is also visually hidden or obscured for most of the chapter, waiting until the final scenes to fully reveal the design.
This is an effective technique for building tension in horror games, as the player is left to imagine what the creature looks like.
