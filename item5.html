#5The Creeper

Minecraft




Creepers from Minecraft are one of the most iconic creatures in gaming, but the design originally came from a mistake.
Originally, the developer was trying to create the pig mob, but mixed up the height and the width, resulting in a creepy design.
This mistake sparked the idea for the creeper, which is now known for being instantly recognizable and terrifying to players.
