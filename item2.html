#2 The Projectionist

Bendy and the Ink Machine




The Projectionist from Bendy and the Ink Machine takes second place due to its ability to highlight the game's unique art style.
The game is set in an animation studio, with the art style reflecting the setting with many references to early animation.
The Projectionist fits right in with the environment, with a stobe light effect shooting from the projector.
