The Reaper Leviathan

Subnautica

The Reaper Leviathan is sea creature from Subnautica, an open-world survival game.
It is known for being the most intimidating part of the game not just for its threatening damage, but also for its appearance.
The game cleverly plays with perspective, making the player only realize how massive the Reaper Levianthan is when it is up close.
This paired with its spider-like facial features and open water environment create a terrifying atmosphere that is more effective than most horror games, despite Subnautica being a survival game.
